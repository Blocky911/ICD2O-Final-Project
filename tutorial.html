ACTIONS
01
Grab the Crown
Use W, A, S, and D to move. Press E to use an item! Tag your opponent to steal the crown from them. Whoever holds the crown earns 10 points per second — that's 1 point every 0.1 seconds.
02
Earn Coins While Playing
Coins are generated passively throughout the match. Spend them wisely — each powerup has a fixed cost and its own cooldown timer.
03
Use Powerups Strategically
Activate powerups at the right moment. Stun your rival with a Rotten Tomato, blast them with a Fart Bomb, or shield yourself with the Untaggable Potion.
04
Watch Your Stamina
Running drains stamina. When empty, you slow to a crawl. Use an Energy Bar to keep sprinting — or time your bursts to stay ahead.
05
Mind the Gummy Bear Crash
The Gummy Bears give a 15s speed surge — but the 8s crash afterward can leave you wide open. Plan for the comedown before you pop one.
06
The Match Lasts 2:30
Each round is two and a half minutes. The player with the most points when the clock hits zero wins. Every second holding the crown counts.
07
Crown Holder Gets Extra Tools
The Untaggable Potion is exclusive to the crown holder. If tagged while active, you absorb the hit and keep the crown — then a brief invincibility window kicks in.
